## 🌐 3 Ways to Deploy Your Website

### Method 1: GitHub Pages via `/docs` (Easiest - 10 Seconds)
The complete production-ready website is already pre-compiled inside the `/docs` folder.

1. On your GitHub repository page, click **Settings** (top tab).
2. Click **Pages** in the left menu.
3. Under **Build and deployment** > **Source**, keep **"Deploy from a branch"**.
4. Under **Branch**:
   - Select **`main`**
   - Select folder **`/docs`** (instead of `/ (root)`)
   - Click **Save**.
5. Your website will be live at `https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/` in about 1 minute!

---

### Method 2: Deploy to Vercel (Recommended - 1 Click & Free)
Vercel is built specifically for modern React/Vite websites and requires zero manual building:

1. Go to [vercel.com](https://vercel.com) and log in with your GitHub account.
2. Click **"Add New Project"** and select your `Badhan Air` repository.
3. Click **"Deploy"** (Vercel automatically detects Vite and builds everything).
4. You receive a free, fast live URL (e.g., `badhan-air.vercel.app`) with free SSL and support for custom domains (`badhanairtravels.com`).

---

### Method 3: Deploy to Netlify (Free & 1 Click)
1. Go to [netlify.com](https://netlify.com) and log in with GitHub.
2. Click **"Add new site"** -> **"Import an existing project"**.
3. Select your repository and click **"Deploy site"**.
